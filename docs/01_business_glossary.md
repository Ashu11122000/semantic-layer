# Business Glossary — E-commerce Semantic Layer

## 1. Purpose

This glossary defines the core business terms used in the e-commerce analytics project. It translates technical database fields into understandable business concepts so that analysts, managers, and dashboard users interpret data consistently.

The source data is stored in PostgreSQL under the `raw` schema.

## 2. Business Terms

| Business Term       | Business Definition                                                                              | Source Tables / Fields                                                                                                              | Notes                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Customer            | A person or organization recorded as a customer of the business.                                 | `raw.customers.customer_id`, `customer_name`, `segment`, `city`, `region`, `signup_date`                                            | `customer_id` uniquely identifies a customer.                                                    |
| Customer Segment    | The business category assigned to a customer, such as Consumer or Corporate.                     | `raw.customers.segment`                                                                                                             | Useful for comparing customer groups.                                                            |
| Region              | The geographic region associated with a customer.                                                | `raw.customers.region`                                                                                                              | Use consistently when comparing geographic performance.                                          |
| Product             | An item offered for sale by the business.                                                        | `raw.products.product_id`, `product_name`, `category`, `unit_price`, `unit_cost`, `active_flag`                                     | Product catalogue prices may differ from the prices recorded at the time of sale.                |
| Order               | A recorded purchase transaction associated with a customer.                                      | `raw.orders.order_id`, `customer_id`, `order_date`, `order_status`                                                                  | An order can contain multiple order items.                                                       |
| Order Status        | The recorded stage or outcome of an order.                                                       | `raw.orders.order_status`                                                                                                           | Revenue reporting must define which statuses are included.                                       |
| Order Item          | A product line associated with an order, including its quantity, sale price, cost, and discount. | `raw.order_items.order_item_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`, `unit_price_at_sale`, `unit_cost_at_sale`, `discount_amount` | Use the sale-time price and cost for historical calculations.                                    |
| Quantity Sold       | The number of units recorded on an order item.                                                   | `raw.order_items.quantity`                                                                                                          | Sum quantities to calculate units sold, subject to the chosen order-status rules.                |
| Unit Selling Price  | The selling price recorded for one unit of a product at the time of sale.                        | `raw.order_items.unit_price_at_sale`                                                                                                | Do not substitute the current catalogue price for historical sales.                              |
| Unit Cost           | The cost recorded for one unit of a product at the time of sale.                                 | `raw.order_items.unit_cost_at_sale`                                                                                                 | Used in a cost-based profit calculation.                                                         |
| Discount            | The discount amount recorded against an order item.                                              | `raw.order_items.discount_amount`                                                                                                   | Confirm whether the source records a line-level or per-unit discount before calculating revenue. |
| Shipping Fee        | The shipping charge recorded on an order.                                                        | `raw.orders.shipping_fee`                                                                                                           | Define whether shipping is included in revenue before publishing a metric.                       |
| Payment             | A record of payment information associated with an order.                                        | `raw.payments.payment_id`, `order_id`, `payment_method`, `payment_status`, `payment_date`                                           | Payment status should be considered when defining collected revenue.                             |
| Return              | A record indicating that an order has a returned item or transaction.                            | `raw.returns.return_id`, `order_id`, `return_date`, `return_reason`                                                                 | The available fields do not identify the returned product or returned quantity.                  |
| Review              | A review score associated with an order.                                                         | `raw.reviews.review_id`, `order_id`, `review_score`, `review_date`                                                                  | The recorded review score ranges from 1 to 5 in the current validation rules.                    |
| Revenue             | The monetary value recognized from qualifying sales under a documented business rule.            | `raw.order_items.quantity`, `unit_price_at_sale`, `discount_amount`; potentially `raw.orders.order_status`                          | The formula and treatment of discounts, order statuses, and shipping must be standardized.       |
| Cost of Goods Sold  | The recorded product cost associated with qualifying units sold.                                 | `raw.order_items.quantity`, `unit_cost_at_sale`                                                                                     | The calculation depends on which orders qualify as sales.                                        |
| Profit              | Revenue less the defined costs included in the metric.                                           | Order-item selling prices, discounts, and unit costs                                                                                | This is a defined sales-profit measure, not necessarily accounting net profit.                   |
| Average Order Value | The average qualifying sales value per distinct order.                                           | `raw.orders.order_id` and the qualifying order-item sales values                                                                    | Calculate from order-level totals, not by averaging individual order-item rows.                  |
| Return Rate         | The proportion of eligible orders that have a return record.                                     | `raw.orders.order_id`, `raw.returns.order_id`                                                                                       | This is an order-based return rate, not a returned-unit rate.                                    |

## 3. Business Rules

1. Customer, order, product, payment, return, review, and order-item identifiers must be preserved as identifiers, not treated as numeric measures.
2. Historical sales calculations must use the recorded sale-time price and cost rather than current catalogue values.
3. Revenue, profit, average order value, and return rate must have documented definitions before they are used in dashboards.
4. Order statuses and payment statuses must be considered explicitly when defining eligible sales.
5. Returns must not automatically be subtracted from revenue unless the chosen metric definition specifies how returns are handled.
6. The source data does not contain an explicit returned quantity or returned product identifier. Unit-based return calculations therefore require additional data or a clearly documented limitation.

## 4. Governance

This glossary is the shared reference for business terminology across SQL views, metric definitions, validation queries, and BI dashboards. If a definition changes, update this document and the corresponding metric governance documentation and tests.
